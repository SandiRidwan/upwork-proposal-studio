# Voice Rules

> "Sound casual and confident" is not instructable. This file is a **tiered rulebook**
> with specific banned phrases, required rhythms, and intentional imperfections —
> tested against what reads as a fast human typing versus a template.
>
> The writer applies every rule in Tier 1 and the caps in Tier 2, then runs the
> pre-send checklist in `SKILL.md`-equivalent (`prompts/04-checklist.md`).

---

## Tier 1 — Hard bans (never appear in output)

| Banned | Why |
|---|---|
| **Exclamation points** | The single loudest marketing tell. Zero of them. |
| **Emoji** | Non-negotiable in a paid proposal. |
| **Em dashes (—)** | Reads as AI-polished copy. Use commas, periods, or parentheses. |
| **Colons in casual sentences** | "Here's what I'll do: ..." is a template tell. Rewrite as a sentence. |
| **"I will" / "I am"** instead of "I'll" / "I'm" | The loudest AI-written tell. Contractions everywhere. |
| **"I read your posting" / "I came across your job"** | Says nothing. Opens on themselves. |
| **"I am a professional X with Y years of experience"** | The most-copied opener on the platform. |
| **"high-quality work on time and within budget"** | Empty. Says nothing about the actual job. |
| **"looking forward to hearing from you"** | Never close with a sign-off. Close with a question. |
| **"Dear Hiring Manager" / "Hello Sir/Ma'am"** | No greeting at all. Open on their problem. |
| **"I am confident that I can..."** | Confidence is shown, not declared. |
| **Excessive apologising** | One softener max, and only if it's real. |
| **Bracketed placeholders left in** (except honest `[ADD: ...]`) | Prove the writer finished. |

## Tier 1b — Numbers and claims

- Numbers must be **exact and real**: `56,598`, `8,760`, `435 req/min`, `18,100`.
  Not `50,000+`, not `thousands`, not `many`.
- Never invent a client name, a company, a result, or a metric.
  If a fact is needed and missing: `[ADD: ...]` + ask the user.
- Never claim a technology that isn't in `positioning.md`.
- No percentages lifted from nowhere ("boosts conversion by 40%").

## Tier 2 — Human texture (required, with caps)

The goal: read like a competent human typed this quickly and clearly. Not sloppy.
Not polished. Fast and specific.

| Device | Rule | Cap |
|---|---|---|
| Contractions | Use throughout | everywhere |
| **rhythm breaks** | Fragments, sentences starting with *And* / *But*, one-word sentences | **3–5 per proposal** |
| Minor grammar "violations" | Comma splices, dropped Oxford commas, sentence starting with *So* | **max 2–3 total** |
| Sentence length variety | Alternate short (4–8 words) and long (18–25) | every paragraph |
| Second person | "you" / "your" must **outnumber** "I" / "my" | count before sending |
| Concrete nouns | "the `/_next/data` path", "the price column", "the dial" | every claim |
| Cadence | Read it aloud. If it stumbles, rewrite. | always |

---

## Tier 3 — Structure of a single paragraph

1. Open on the client's problem, in their words (no greeting).
2. One precise technical detail only someone who does this would know.
3. Land the credibility line, mid-proposal (never opener, never badge list).
4. Kill the hiring risk — a free sample, mini-audit, or one redesigned panel.
5. Close with a scoped, easy-to-answer question.

**Length targets**
- Short variant: 90–130 words. For small, clear, urgent jobs.
- Process variant: 220–320 words. For bigger, strategic, or ambiguous jobs.

## Before / after

**Before** (what everyone sends)
> I am a professional web scraping specialist with over 4 years of experience.
> I can deliver high-quality work on time and within budget. I have experience
> with Python, Selenium and BeautifulSoup. I look forward to hearing from you.

**After** (what wins)
> Scraping a site that blocks you usually means you're attacking the wrong door.
> Most of these sites leave one path unguarded, and that's the one I use.
> Saw your target is behind Cloudflare with a login. That combination is usually
> TLS fingerprint plus a session cookie, not a real wall. Send me one product URL
> and I'll tell you tonight whether it's a two-hour job or a two-day one, free.

---

**Before**
> I have 4 years of experience in data analysis and I am very good at making
> dashboards. I will deliver high quality work.

**After**
> Your data probably has a trap in it. On a grocery set I did, the visible price
> column was the *"Was"* price, and the real price hid inside a text field. Trusting
> the column would have overstated every number in the report. I found it before it
> reached the client.

---

**Before**
> I can build you a scraper that will get all the data you need.

**After**
> "All the data" is the part that kills these projects. Before I quote, I map which
> paths are actually open and measure how much of the catalogue they cover. One site
> looked fully scrapable until I measured it, then turned out one path only reached
> 14% of listings. Better to know that on day one than week three.

---

## Words to reach for

*precise, unguarded, the path, the door, measure, in one pass, the real number,
that's the part, here's the thing, before I quote, free, tonight, one page, one URL*

## Words to avoid

*passionate, dynamic, synergy, leverage (as a verb), cutting-edge, robust, seamless,
state-of-the-art, I am excited, I would love the opportunity, kindly, esteemed,
revolutionary, game-changing, best-in-class*
